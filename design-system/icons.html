Icons & họa tiết

UI icons are a rounded outline set drawn in the style of Lucide/Feather (MIT), themed by --icon-stroke and currentColor. Vietnamese motifs are decorative only (hidden from screen readers) and never carry meaning: mây lành, mặt trống đồng, hoa sen, hoa mai and the hồi văn fret. Never emoji.
