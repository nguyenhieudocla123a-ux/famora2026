FamilyHub Design System

Like a family altar: a black-lacquer band with gold leaf and a turning bronze drum, and a tear-off calendar sheet in đỏ son on dó paper, so the lunar days that matter are seen first. Đỏ son leads every action; black lacquer marks solemn days (ngày giỗ); gold leaf only draws lines and rings. Toggle Dev Mode to inspect a component; Copy as prompt implements it in React Native.

Palette

Semantic roles, each fill paired with its --on-* foreground; ratios are WCAG-computed from the hex.

Typography

Be Vietnam Pro is the reading face (full Vietnamese diacritics, open counters). Playfair Display appears only at h1–h3 and the big lunar numeral. Body starts at 17px for older readers.

Spacing

4/8 grid plus semantic roles: related (8) < section (24) < major (32); gutter 20; touch target 48.

Shape

Soft corners. The petal radius (one sharp corner) is reserved for the single feature card on a screen.

Shadows

Soft red-tinted shadow, no border stacked on top. Four levels are used; MUI's 0–24 collapses onto them.

Transitions

Prominent but purposeful. Content rises in a 70ms stagger; controls answer with a spring; ambient motifs drift for 9–40s. Everything rests under "Reduce motion".

Icons & motifs

Rounded outline set in the style of Lucide (MIT), stroke-width: var(--icon-stroke). Motifs are decorative and hidden from screen readers. Full sheet: icons.html.

Trống đồng 3D

Procedural Đông Sơn drum (three.js, drum3d.js): lathe body, canvas-engraved face with the 14-ray star, circle-dot and tick bands and 18 Lạc birds, aged bronze with patina. Turns slowly; strike() spins it faster with the drum sound. Still under Reduce Motion; the SVG drum is the fallback when WebGL is unavailable. Needs the page served over http (python -m http.server).

Âm thanh

Synthesized in tools/synth_sounds.py (no samples, no licences). Off by default; the user turns them on in Settings. Never autoplay.
